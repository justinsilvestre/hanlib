多言無益
Uselessness of Many Words
`>[of] 多 ^many - 言 ^words / [^the] 無益 without-advantage (^useless[ness])

或問墨子曰。
Someone asked Mo-tzu,
`或 someone / 問 ask[ed] / 墨子 a_surname-philosopher (Mo\-tzu) , 曰 speak[:saying] ,`

言以多爲貴乎。
"Is it from quantity that words become precious?"
`[^do] 言 words / 2以 through - 多 quantity / 爲 become / 1貴 precious / 乎 interrogative_particle (~\?)`

墨子曰。蝦蟆日夜鳴。而人厭之。
Mo-tzu said, "Frogs croak day and night, yet men loathe them.
`墨子 a_surname-philosopher (Mo\-tzu) / 曰 speak[:said] ,_" 蝦蟆 frog-frog ([~]^frog[s]) / >日 day - [and] 夜 night / 鳴 cry[:croak] , 而 yet / 人 man[:men] / 厭 loathe / 之 them.`

雄鷄一鳴。天下振動。
(But when) the cock crows (only) once, every thing (lit. under the sky) comes into motion.
`[^but when the] 雄鷄 male_of_birds-fowl (cock) / >[only] 一 once / 鳴 cry[:crows] , 天下 sky-under (everything) / 振動 excite-move (come[s]_into_motion).`

言在當時。而已。
It is important to speak at the proper time, and that is all.
`言 ^words / 在 rely [on] / [the] 當時 proper-time (proper_time) , [and] 而已 and-finished (that_is_all).`

多言何。
What is the good of talking much?"
`>多 much / >言 words[:talking] / 何 what [is the good of]?"`
